## Visual language
- Pure white page (#FFFFFF), near-black ink (#111), one accent: terracotta (~#D2694F). Nothing else.
- Type: a high-contrast book serif (Copernicus/Tiempos-like), lowercase words, tight tracking; weight can animate (think: 500 -> 900).
- Images: public-domain engravings, anatomical plates, marble sculpture photos, all greyscale on white (blend: multiply). Every image carries a small **red dot** (the period of the sentence) and a **black ink line** (arc, ring, bracket, underline) that "points" at something.
- Motion: hard cuts every 0.1-0.15 s in the montage, push/blur on the type cuts, hand-drawn ink arcs that grow into rings or into the letter C, the asterisk drawn like a brush stroke then extruded in 3D.
- The film ends where it starts: asterisk + serif "Claude" wordmark (0-0.3 s and 13.0-14.4 s).
