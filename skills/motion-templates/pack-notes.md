## Hooks & Captions pack (20 mogrts, 1080x1920)
- Fonts named by the author: Neue Haas Grotesk (https://fonts.adobe.com/fonts/neue-haas-grotesk) and Playfair (https://fonts.adobe.com/fonts/playfair). Neither is needed here: use Inter / Playfair Display from Google Fonts.
- Preview music credits (Envato / AudioJungle) are listed in the pack's `Music Used in Preview.txt`; the music is not part of the pack.

## Tutorial.mp4 (75s, no narration, on-screen text only)
How the author says the templates are used in Premiere:
1. Go to the Graphics Templates panel and drag-drop the .mogrt onto the timeline.
2. Colours, texts and other properties are controlled from the Properties (Essential Graphics) panel.
3. Add media through the hamburger icon and "Replace from finder".
In our harness the equivalent controls are the template's "Editable controls" table in `templates/<slug>.md`.
