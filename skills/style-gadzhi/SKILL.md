# Gadzhi-style playbook

Values live in `styles/gadzhi.json`; this file says how to apply them.

- **Pacing**: no dead air. Silences over ~0.35s are cut. Aim for a visual change every 2-4 seconds.
- **Captions**: 3 words per page, uppercase, heavy stroke, bottom third. Pop-in per word.
- **Emphasis**: highlight (yellow) numbers, money, outcomes and contrast words ("never", "only", "but"). At most 1-2 per page.
- **Punch-ins**: 1.10-1.15 scale, ~1.2s, roughly every 4-7s, landing on an emphasis word or a new point. Never two overlapping.
- **Don't**: stack many effects at once, cover the face with captions, zoom past 1.2, or use more than 2 colours in captions.

Not built yet (add via `add-component`): B-roll cutaways, stat counters, SFX/whooshes, text-behind-subject.
